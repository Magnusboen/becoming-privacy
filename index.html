Privacy Policy for Becoming

Last updated: April 25, 2026

Overview

Becoming is a personal transformation app designed to help you build identity-based habits through daily reflection and conscious living. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

Who we are

Becoming is developed and operated by Magnus Bøen. If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you can contact us at: becomingapp.contact@gmail.com

What data we collect

When you use Becoming, we collect the following:

  • Account information — your name, collected during onboarding and stored securely
  • Apple Sign In — we use Apple Sign In for authentication. We receive a unique user identifier from Apple but do not receive your Apple ID email unless you choose to share it
  • Identity data — your identity statement, why statement, and motivations that you enter in the app
  • Daily wins — the habits and wins you set up and track
  • Check-in data — your daily reflections, scores, notes, and win completions
  • Notification settings — your preferred times for reminders and evening reflection
  • Saved quotes — quotes you save in the Wisdom tab
  • Streak data — your current and longest streaks

How we use your data

All data you enter is used solely to provide the core functionality of the app — showing you your identity, tracking your wins, and building your streak history. We do not sell your data, share it with third parties, or use it for advertising purposes.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a PostgreSQL database platform with industry-standard security practices. All data is associated with your unique user ID and protected by row-level security policies.

Third party services

Becoming uses the following third party services:

  • Supabase — database and authentication (supabase.com/privacy)
  • Apple Sign In — authentication (apple.com/legal/privacy)
  • Expo — app build and notification infrastructure (expo.dev/privacy)

Data retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all associated data will be permanently deleted from our systems.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data by contacting us at becomingapp.contact@gmail.com

Children's privacy

Becoming is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes through the app.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or your data, please contact us at: becomingapp.contact@gmail.com
