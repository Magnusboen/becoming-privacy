Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 23, 2026

What we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have about it.

01Overview

Becoming is a personal transformation app that helps you build identity-based habits through daily reflection and conscious living. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, where it's stored, and the rights you have over it.

02Who we are

Becoming is developed and operated by Magnus Bøen ("we," "us"), based in Norway. Magnus Bøen is the data controller responsible for your personal data under this policy.

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to becomingapp.contact@gmail.com.

03What data we collect

When you use Becoming, we collect the following:

  • Account information — your name, collected during onboarding and stored securely.
  • Sign-in data — we offer Sign in with Apple and Sign in with Google. Depending on which you choose, we receive a unique user identifier and, if you allow it, your name and email address. With Apple Sign In, you can choose to hide your real email address, in which case we only receive a private relay address.
  • Identity data — the identity statement, "why" statement, and motivations you enter in the app.
  • Daily wins — the habits and wins you set up and track.
  • Check-in data — your daily reflections, scores, notes, and win completions.
  • Streak data — your current and longest streaks.
  • Saved quotes — quotes you save in the Wisdom tab.
  • Notification data — your preferred reminder and evening reflection times, and a device push token used to deliver those notifications.
  • Subscription data — your subscription status, entitlements, and purchase history, managed on our behalf by RevenueCat and processed by Apple or Google as the payment provider. We do not receive or store your card details.

04How we use your data

All data you enter is used solely to provide the core functionality of the app — showing you your identity, tracking your wins, building your streak history, sending the reminders you set, and managing your subscription. We do not sell your data, share it for advertising, or use it to build advertising profiles.

We process this data on the basis of: performing our contract with you (delivering the app's core features and your subscription), and our legitimate interest in operating and improving the app securely.

05Where your data is stored

Your data is stored using Supabase, a PostgreSQL database platform with industry-standard security practices. All data is associated with your unique user ID and protected by row-level security policies, meaning your account can only access its own data.

07Data retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all associated data — including your profile, identity data, check-ins, streaks, and authentication record — is permanently deleted from our systems.

08Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have some or all of the following rights over your personal data:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction — request that inaccurate or incomplete data be corrected.
  • Deletion — request deletion of your data, or delete your account directly from within the app.
  • Portability — request your data in a structured, commonly used format.
  • Restriction or objection — ask us to limit how we process your data, or object to certain processing.
  • Complaint — if you're in the EEA or Norway, lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. In Norway, this is Datatilsynet.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at becomingapp.contact@gmail.com. We'll respond within a reasonable timeframe.

09Children's privacy

Becoming is not intended for children under 13, and in some countries the applicable minimum age may be higher (for example, 16 in parts of the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect data from children below the applicable minimum age for their country. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

10Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app or in applicable law. We'll notify you of any significant changes through the app, and the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent revision.

11Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or how your data is handled, reach out at becomingapp.contact@gmail.com.
